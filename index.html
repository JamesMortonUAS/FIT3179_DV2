WHY PLACE MATTERSDESIGN NOTE

Electricity is produced differently across the country

In the 2025 workbook, renewables supplied 97.5% of Tasmania’s generation and 77.0% of South Australia’s, against 31.3% in Queensland and 21.4% in Western Australia.

These are shares of generation within each state. They do not say how much electricity a state generated, imported or consumed.

INTERPRETING THE MAPSDIFFERENT MEASURES

Three maps, three questions

The shaded map shows renewable share. The circle map compares total electricity generated by state. The point map shows where major stations are located.

Capacity is a station's rated power in megawatts. Generation measures energy produced over a year in gigawatt-hours. A large station need not produce the most energy.
